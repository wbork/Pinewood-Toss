  • Level up on the blue XP creatures drop. Each level, pick a new weapon (carry up to four) or an upgrade.
  • Fuse two weapons that work together by maxing both: they become one much stronger weapon and free a slot.
  • Coins buy chests. A chest's glow shows its rarity; rarer items are stronger.
  • Glowing creatures are about to use their ability: dodge the pounce, the charge, the bolt.
  • Bosses arrive at 5:00 and 10:00. Step out of red circles and jump shockwaves.
  • After the Hollow King the forest rages: every kill and every second scores points until you fall.
